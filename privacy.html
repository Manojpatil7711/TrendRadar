POLICY

Privacy, plainly.

This is an early beta. We aim to collect as little personal information as practical.

Information stored in your browser

Saved watchlist topics are stored locally in your browser using local storage. They are not synced to an account because this beta has no user accounts.

Browser notifications

Notifications are optional. The browser asks for permission first. This beta can show watchlist-match notifications while the site is open; it does not provide background push alerts while the site is closed.

Public feeds

When you load the trend dashboard, the service requests public RSS feeds from Google Trends and Google News through a server-side endpoint. The original feed/source links are shown where available. Availability and contents depend on those providers.

Analytics and advertising

This initial beta does not intentionally add advertising trackers or an analytics SDK. Hosting providers may process standard request and security logs as part of operating their services.
