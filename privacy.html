POLICY

Privacy, plainly.

We aim to collect only the information needed to provide accounts and membership access.

Account information

For Google sign-in, we store your email address, Google account identifier, account creation time, and a hashed session token with its expiry. The backend also contains legacy email/password endpoints; if those are used, a salted password hash is stored instead of the plain-text password. The current account page uses Google sign-in and does not request your name, phone number, Gmail, Drive, contacts, or other profile access.

Membership and payments

If you buy Pro after checkout is enabled, we store your plan status, membership start and expiry times, payment/order identifiers and transaction status so access can be activated and checked. Payment processing is handled by Razorpay. TrendRadar does not store your full card number, CVV, UPI PIN or banking password.

Sessions and security

Login sessions use a secure, HttpOnly cookie and expire automatically. We use basic safeguards to reduce repeated password guesses. Do not share your password. Password reset and self-service account deletion are not yet available; contact support for account/privacy requests.

Information stored in your browser

Saved watchlist topics and alert preferences may be stored locally in your browser. They are not automatically synced to your account in this beta.

Public feeds

When you load the dashboard, the service requests public RSS feeds from Google Trends and Google News through a server-side endpoint. Original source links are shown where available. Availability and contents depend on those providers.

Analytics and advertising

Display ads are not enabled at this stage. If advertising is added later, free access may show ads while active Pro membership will remain ad-free. Any advertising integration and related policy disclosures will be updated before ads are enabled. Hosting providers may process standard request and security logs.
